FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to create an account? ▼
No account needed at all. Just go to the app, type a task, and get your steps. If you upgrade to Pro, your email is your access key — no password to forget.
How is this different from just asking ChatGPT? ▼
FocusStep is specifically designed for ADHD brains. It enforces strict limits — 3 to 5 steps maximum, one action per step, hyper-specific detail. ChatGPT gives you paragraphs. We give you actions.
What if I'm still stuck after getting the steps? ▼
Tap "I need help with this step" on any step. You can ask a question or pick from three suggested options. You'll get 2-3 even smaller sub-steps for just that one action.
Can I cancel my subscription? ▼
Yes — cancel anytime from within the app with one click. No emails, no phone calls, no hassle. Your Pro access continues until the end of your billing period.
Is my data private? ▼
Your tasks are processed to generate steps and not stored permanently on our servers. History and favourites are saved locally on your device.
